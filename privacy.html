Privacy policy: automation-toolkit

Last updated October 30, 2026

automation-toolkit is a personal app used only by its owner. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): recent inbox messages and whether a reply was sent.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): the list of calendars and today's events.
  • Google Drive (only files this app creates): one folder and the summary files it writes there. It can't see any other Drive files.

How the data is used

The app builds a short text summary and saves it to the owner's own Google Drive. It does not send, change or delete email or calendar events. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app keeps no copy of the data anywhere except the summary file in the owner's Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the developer contact email shown on this app's Google consent screen, or via github.com/Son116net.
